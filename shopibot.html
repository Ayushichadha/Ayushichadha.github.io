Building ShopiBot.

A domain-specific assistant for Shopify developers working on client projects and everyday implementation questions.

Ownership and product direction

I proposed the idea and led the product design, engineering, and testing. The goal was to support developers with technical questions, requirements understanding, and implementation guidance grounded in the Shopify domain.

I independently built the retrieval and question-answering pipeline in the early months, then onboarded two developers as the system grew.

Grounding a general-purpose model

We used hosted language models, with retrieval supplying domain context. The work focused on query analysis and how different kinds of questions should move through different retrieval pipelines.

I read and experimented with relevant research, including RAPTOR and corrective and adaptive retrieval approaches. We used LangChain and LangSmith, alongside experiments with LlamaIndex. The development process involved comparing methods, testing responses, and working through the trade-offs of a usable product.

Engineering and business

I worked with the CEO on technical direction and business discussions, built demonstrations for prospective clients and US startup partnerships, and gave internal seminars supporting the move from robotic process automation toward LLM-based automation.

Balancing research with delivery was an important part of this work: understanding what was worth exploring, what could be built reliably, and how it served the developer using it.

Earlier automation and applied AI

My earlier work included a Page-Speed Analyzer MVP and automation with Robocorp. Machine learning R&D included DOM-tree pattern mining and feedback for checkout-flow testing. I also worked on knowledge-graph and multi-tool workflows, and an image-generation system using CLIP-guided prompts, DreamBooth, and Stable Diffusion.

Building from first principles

Alongside this work, I studied Karpathy’s educational material and built a minimal autograd engine, bigram and trigram models, and a GPT-style language model with a custom tokeniser. Later, I implemented matrix multiplication, GELU, and layer normalisation in C and CUDA.
